# Ubang Brand Assets

The Subpool Ubang variant uses the official raster marks loaded by the public
Ubang website.

| Repository asset | Source | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `web/public/brand/ubang-logo-light.png` | `https://ubang.cn/logo@3x.png` | Dark surfaces |
| `web/public/brand/ubang-logo-blue.png` | `https://ubang.cn/assets/home/logo_introduce@3x.png` | Light surfaces |

Both source images are 519 by 195 pixels with transparency. Preserve their
aspect ratio and do not recolor or redraw the marks.
